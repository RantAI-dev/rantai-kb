# A. Training recipes & SOTA for small visual document retrievers (2025–2026)

Research agent report, compiled 2026-11-18. All numbers have a primary-source URL next to them. Benchmarks: ViDoRe v1 (10 English sets), v2 (4 multilingual), v3 (8 professional domains, incl. French) — NDCG@5 unless noted; ViDoRe V3 (NVIDIA/vidore leaderboard variant) and MIRACL-VISION (18 languages, 7,898 human queries, https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11651) — NDCG@10.

Near-misses (context): **NanoVDR** students are text-only (69–151M) but the document side stays a 2B teacher ([arXiv:2603.12824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12824)). **Vultron Retriever Flash 0.8B**: v3 nDCG@10 0.565, v2 0.604, v1 0.882 ([NeoMME blog table](https://huggingface.co/blog/Hcompany/neomme), MTEB-sourced). **NeoMME-800M**: v3 0.556 ([blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/Hcompany/neomme)). **jina-embeddings-v4** has **no <500M variant** — it is a 3.75B Qwen2.5-VL model with 60M LoRA adapters, 2048-d single + 128-d multi-vector, Matryoshka 128–2048, 30+ languages, ViDoRe v1 90.4 / v2 60.1 ([card](https://huggingface.co/jinaai/jina-embeddings-v4), [ModernVBERT table](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01149v3)). **NVIDIA**: the only Nemotron VL embedders are 1B/8B (see §4); no sub-500M Nemotron retriever exists as of Nov 2026 ([collection](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B-BF16)).

**Key structural finding**: from-scratch single-vector at small scale collapses (BiModernVBERT 37.4 vs ColModernVBERT 81.2 on v1, a 17.6-point gap; 20.3 on v2 — [ModernVBERT/DistilVDR](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)), while **distilled** single-vector at 524M (DistilVDR) beats every sub-1B multi-vector baseline (61.74 vs colSmol-500M's 53.01 average) with a 15.6× smaller index (16.4 GB vs 256 GB per 1M pages) and ~100× faster scoring ([DistilVDR](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)).

## 2. Distillation methods for embedding models

**Pointwise embedding-space (cosine/regression) alignment.** The student regresses onto cached teacher vectors, L = 1 − cos(v_s, v_t). NanoVDR compared six objectives across 3 backbones × 22 ViDoRe sets: **pure cosine alignment beat KL-ranking by +1.1/+4.0/+2.5 NDCG@5 (v1/v2/v3) and beat hard-label InfoNCE by +10.7/+21.6/+14.1** ([arXiv:2603.12824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12824)). Teacher quality predicts retention (r=+0.607); student–teacher cosine does not (r=+0.094) — the *geometry* of the teacher space is the signal. DistilVDR confirms: adding InfoNCE or KL on top of cosine alignment gives no gain (InfoNCE −0.12…−0.50; KL flat ±0.14) ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10636)). NVIDIA used the same recipe with MSE added: Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B was "distilled from the 8B teacher using combined cosine distance (COS) and mean squared error (MSE) losses", LM kept frozen ([model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B-BF16)).

**Cross-modal distillation.** NanoVDR: a frozen 2B VLM teacher (Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B, 2048-d) embeds documents offline; a 69M DistilBERT + 2-layer MLP projector (768→2048) maps *text queries* into the teacher's image-document space. Student never sees an image; retrieval is plain cosine against teacher page vectors. Result: **92.4% teacher retention (95.1% with multilingual query augmentation), beating 2B DSE-Qwen2 on v2/v3 with 32× fewer params, 50× lower CPU query latency (51 ms vs 2.5 s)**, trained in **<13 GPU-hours** ([arXiv:2603.12824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12824)). The modality gap is *not* the bottleneck — cross-lingual transfer is: per-language retention English 94.3%, French 92.1%, Italian/Spanish ~90%, German 85.7%, **Portuguese (unseen) 75.6%**; fixed cheaply by machine-translating queries only.

**Dual-student (bilateral) distillation.** DistilVDR distils *both* sides from one frozen 8B teacher (4096-d): doc student 454M (InternViT-300M tiles + ModernBERT-base over ≤7,168 visual tokens), query student 70M DistilBERT. **86.9% of teacher average (61.74 vs 71.05), 15× smaller end-to-end, single-vector, teacher discarded after training**; gap decomposition: doc side costs 6.03, query side 4.69 of 9.31 points ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10636)).

**Size ratios where students keep most of the teacher:**
- 29× (2B→69M), 92–95% retention, cross-modal — NanoVDR ([arXiv:2603.12824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12824))
- 15× (8B→524M), 84–87% retention, end-to-end — DistilVDR ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10636))
- ~8× (8B→1.55B), 96% (ViDoRe V3 image 58.02 vs 60.39) — Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B-BF16))
- 4.7× (109M→23M text embedder), full teacher-space compatibility, no labels/hard negatives — LEAF ([arXiv:2509.12539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12539)); LEAF students inherit Matryoshka truncation and output-quantization robustness from the teacher
- Text-side precursors: EmbedDistill distils the teacher's *geometry* rather than scores (Kim et al. 2023, cited in [NanoVDR §2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12824v3)); TAS-B/MarginMSE/RankDistil are the score/KL lineage (same source).

**Matryoshka & projection heads.** DistilVDR's teacher is MRL-trained, so truncating the 4096-d target to 768-d is principled: index shrinks 5.3× (16.4→3.07 GB/1M) but costs −3.19 avg NDCG@5, −4.77 on v3 ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)). Nemotron-3.5-Embed supports truncating to first 1024/512 dims with re-normalisation ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B-BF16)). EVIE uses prefix-MRL so one checkpoint serves six nested widths without re-encoding ([arXiv:2610.11553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11553)). A 2-layer MLP projector (hidden 768 → teacher dim) is the standard head in NanoVDR/DistilVDR; a single linear (768→4096) sufficed for DistilVDR's query tower ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)).

**Score/KL and listwise variants** still matter when the teacher space is weak or the student is multi-vector: EVIE's bidirectional teacher–student with *symmetric listwise distillation* pushes EVIE-8B to 66.75 nDCG@10 on ViDoRe V3, +1.43 over the best external baseline ([arXiv:2610.11553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11553)). CLIP-KD/TinyCLIP combine feature alignment + affinity mimicking for CLIP compression (image classification, not VDR) (cited in [NanoVDR §2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12824v3)).

## 3. Efficiency techniques for page images

**Visual token compression.**
- Pixel-shuffle r=4: ModernVBERT compresses each 512² tile's 1,024 patches to 64 tokens ([paper §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01149v3)).
- Tile budgets: DistilVDR caps pages at 6×448² tiles + thumbnail (HiRes) or 2 (Fast). 0 tiles→6 tiles: +5.12 avg NDCG@5 (v3 41.0→47.1); Fast→HiRes +1.76 avg / +3.41 v3 at 3× tokens ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)).
- Post-hoc multi-vector compression: Light-ColPali/ColQwen retains **98.2%** of retrieval quality with reduced patch embeddings ([arXiv:2506.04997](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04997)); MAGIC (training-free optimal-transport compressor) improves the accuracy–budget trade-off most at low retained-vector budgets ([arXiv:2609.21018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21018)); MarginMerge does coverage-guided merging ([arXiv:2608.02969](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02969v1)); query-aware token budgeting (BCR) on ColModernVBERT ([arXiv:2609.07262](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07262)).
- EVIE's HAC (hierarchical agglomerative clustering with spatial regularisation, store centroids): EVIE-4.5B keeps **59.58 nDCG@10 at 3.81 GiB per million pages, 128× payload reduction** ([arXiv:2610.11553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11553)).

**Low resolution vs quality.** ModernVBERT's controlled sweep (doc-retrieval aggregate): 512px→30.7, 1024px→42.2, 2048px→43.8, 2048px+high-res cooldown→**45.8**; natural-image tasks *degrade* with resolution ([paper Tab.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01149v3)). DistilVDR shows the same on v3 (dense small text): 0 tiles 41.0 vs 6 tiles 47.1 ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)).

**Late interaction vs single vector.** From-scratch: +38 v1-points for multi-vector (ColModernVBERT 81.2 vs BiModernVBERT 37.4; 17.6-point gap stated in [DistilVDR §1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)). Cost: ~1,000×128-d fp16 vectors/page = 256 GB per 1M pages and MaxSim scoring ~1.2 s/10K docs vs 9.6 ms for one 4096-d dot product (sub-1B scale; [DistilVDR Tab.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)). Distillation closes most of the quality gap at single-vector cost (DistilVDR 61.74 vs ColModernVBERT 42.46 sub-1B average). NeoMME offers a hybrid: dense head for ANN recall + late-interaction rerank from the same forward pass ([blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/Hcompany/neomme)).

**Vector quantisation.** NeoMME-260M on ViDoRe v3: hierarchical token pooling (factor 10) + int8 → 39× smaller (1.5 MB→39 kB/page) keeping **>99%** nDCG@10; pooling 8 + int8 queries + **binary documents** → 6 kB/page (255×) keeping **>95%** ([blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/Hcompany/neomme), method from [mixedbread asymmetric quant](https://www.mixedbread.com/blog/asymmetric-quant)). Single-vector side: LEAF students inherit robustness to output quantization ([arXiv:2509.12539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12539)); Matryoshka truncation costs are quantified above.

**CPU-specific numbers** (our deployment regime): NanoVDR-S 69M query encode 51 ms on 1 CPU thread; ColModernVBERT 183 ms; DSE-Qwen2 2,539 ms ([NanoVDR Tab.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12824v3)). ColModernVBERT author-measured 20 ms/query on CPU ([ModernVBERT Tab.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01149v3)) and 5,000 queries/s batched on H100. DistilVDR query tower: DistilBERT 2.07 ms vs ModernBERT 10.61 ms at B=1 ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)).

## 4. Training data recipes and compute

- **ColPali lineage** (colSmol, ColFlor, NanoVDR, DistilVDR): 127,460 query–page pairs; 63% academic train sets + 37% synthetic VLM-generated pseudo-questions (Claude-3 Sonnet); 2% validation; LoRA r=32/α=32, paged AdamW 8-bit, lr 5e-4, 3 epochs ([colSmol-500M card](https://huggingface.co/vidore/colSmol-500M)).
- **NanoVDR**: 711K pairs after dedup — VisRAG-Synthetic 239K, ColPali train 111K, VisRAG-InDomain 96K, VDR-Multilingual 280K (en/es/it/de/fr); **machine-translated queries** (Helsinki-NLP OpusMT, 489K queries × 5 languages, ~200K/lang) → 1.49M pairs. OneCycleLR, peak 2e-4, eff. batch 1024, 20 epochs, **10.1 GPU-hours on one H200 + ~1 GPU-hour teacher caching = <13 GPU-hours total**. Data efficiency: 25% of data already gives 93/82/70% retention (v1/v2/v3) ([arXiv:2603.12824](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12824v3)).
- **DistilVDR**: 1.20M unique images (711K base + 454K multi-domain + 31.7K finance supplement), perceptual-hash dedup against all ViDoRe corpora; queries = the 1.49M NanoVDR set; cached 4096-d teacher targets cost **99.5 H200-GPU-hours (11.3 images/s)**; students train independently against cached targets (per-tower hours not stated — UNVERIFIED) ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)).
- **ModernVBERT**: modality alignment ~2B tokens (Cauldron 2 + Docmatix upsampled for documents; final model 10B tokens + 2B high-res cooldown); contrastive post-training: 118K ColPali pairs + 118K MSCOCO, interleaving **text-only pairs at 2:1 text:image ratio** (+1.7 nDCG@5 on doc retrieval), 1–2 hard negatives per pair, task-aware batching; total **≈2,000 H100 GPU-hours** ([arXiv:2510.01149](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01149v3)).
- **Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B**: 2.3M image(+text) samples + 8.5M text-only; public sets incl. ColPali-PDF, Docmatix/docmatix-ir, wiki-ss-nq, vdr-multilingual; synthetic queries generated by Nemotron-3, Qwen3.x, gpt-oss, Pixtral-12B ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B-BF16)).
- **EVIE**: evidence-judged data governance — a multimodal judge filters answer-bearing positives and unreliable negatives before training ([arXiv:2610.11553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11553), [GitHub](https://github.com/Tencent/EVIE)).
- **NeoMME**: from-scratch masked-diffusion pretraining on 524B packed tokens (290B text-only), NorMuon optimizer for data efficiency ([HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/Hcompany/neomme)).

## What I'd recommend for our case

- **Distil, don't train from scratch.** From-scratch single-vector at <500M collapses (BiModernVBERT 37.4 v1); distilled single-vector at 524M reaches 61.74 avg and beats every sub-1B multi-vector model ([DistilVDR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10636)). Our Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B teacher is already single-vector (2048-d, mean-pooled) — exactly the format both NanoVDR and DistilVDR succeeded with.
- **Use pointwise cosine alignment (optionally +MSE) as the primary loss; skip contrastive terms and hard negatives.** Both NanoVDR and DistilVDR ablations show it dominates, and it needs no negatives/labels — teacher caches only ([arXiv:2603.12824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12824), [arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10636), [Nemotron card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B-BF16)).
- **Distil bilaterally, asymmetrically**: a ~70M multilingual text query tower (XLM-R/DistilBERT-class) with a linear/2-layer MLP projector into the teacher's 2048-d space, and a vision-heavy document tower (InternViT-300M-448 tiles + a bidirectional encoder) — the DistilVDR blueprint at 524M total.
- **Our student can and should be smaller on the query side**: NanoVDR showed 69M ≈ 151M for query encoding (82.2 vs 82.4 v1).
- **Indonesian coverage must be engineered via the query side**: cross-lingual transfer was NanoVDR's *primary* bottleneck; retention tracks training-query language share (Portuguese, unseen: 75.6%). Since alignment training is query-only, Indonesian needs only machine-translated/synthetic Indonesian queries re-encoded by the teacher — cheap, no new images ([arXiv:2603.12824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12824)). The teacher itself is evaluated on Indonesian for both text (34 langs) and image (18 langs) ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B-BF16)).
- **Budget: ~1–1.5M query–page pairs, perceptual-hash dedup against eval corpora.** NanoVDR's mixture (VisRAG-Synthetic + ColPali + VisRAG-InDomain + VDR-Multilingual) is a proven starting mix; 25% of it already retains 93/82/70% (v1/v2/v3).
- **Tile pages at 448px into ≤3–7 tiles + one thumbnail; don't go below ~2 tiles.** 0→6 tiles costs 5.12 avg NDCG@5; 2→6 tiles costs 3.41 on v3 only ([arXiv:2608.10636](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10636v1)). Offer a Fast (2-tile) and HiRes (6-tile) variant sharing weights.
- **Target compute on the DGX Spark is realistic**: <13 GPU-hours (query tower, NanoVDR) + ~100 H200-hours-class teacher caching at 1.2M images (DistilVDR, 11.3 img/s) — cache once, reuse for all ablations; teacher runs exactly once.
- **Pick a bidirectional encoder over a causal decoder for the student towers** (+10.6 v1 NDCG@5 for bidirectional×late interaction; encoder-only beats causal decoder at equal size in DistilVDR's baselines) ([ModernVBERT §3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01149v3)).
- **Keep the single 2048-d vector; make it Matryoshka-trained** so a 768-d/512-d prefix is a valid embedding for cheap indexes (Nemotron already supports end-truncation; DistilVDR quantifies the cost: −3.19 avg for 768-d).
- **Quantise document vectors asymmetrically at serving**: int8 or binary documents with float queries kept >99%/>95% nDCG@10 for NeoMME; expect similar for single vectors.
- **Evaluate on ViDoRe v1/v2/v3 + MIRACL-VISION** (18 langs incl. Indonesian, human queries) — the teacher's own eval sets — plus our own Indonesian page corpus; report *retention vs teacher*, the metric both distillation papers use.
- **Watch the 2026 frontier**: NeoMME-260M-Retriever (v3 nDCG@10 0.523 at 260M, Apache-2.0) and Vultron Flash 0.8B are the strongest sub-1B from-scratch baselines to beat or borrow from; EVIE's listwise+Prefix-MRL recipe is the upgrade path if cosine alignment saturates.
- **Peregrine implication**: a distilled student is encoder-only BERT-style towers + MLP head + mean pooling — the same class Peregrine already runs (BERT/XLM-R/ModernBERT); no VLM decoding needed at inference, which is what makes the 10–30× CPU speedup vs the 25 s/page teacher plausible.

## Open questions

- No published CPU *document-side* latency for any distilled VDR student (all doc-throughput numbers are GPU). Our 2-core Xeon measurement of Nemotron (~25 s/page) has no direct analogue in the literature; we must measure DistilVDR-style towers on our CPUs ourselves.
- DistilVDR's per-tower student training GPU-hours (beyond the 99.5-h teacher cache) are not stated in what I read — UNVERIFIED.
- ViDoRe v3's French and MIRACL-VISION's Indonesian are the only non-English page-retrieval evals found; no Indonesian-specific page-retrieval benchmark exists — we likely need to build a small labelled set (e.g., translated ViDoRe-style queries over Indonesian PDFs).
- Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B's distillation kept the text LM frozen and only trained vision+projection — whether full fine-tuning of a smaller student into the same space does better at <500M is untested publicly.
- How well does cosine-alignment distillation transfer when the teacher is a *Pixtral-style* encoder (Nemotron) rather than the Qwen3-VL lineage all published distillations used? NanoVDR found teacher quality predicts retention (r=+0.607), which is encouraging, but it's a single-family observation.
- Latency numbers for NeoMME/ColModernVBERT are query-side or GPU-index-side; ARM/AVX-512 page-encoding throughput for any of these models is UNVERIFIED.
