# Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. Its surface is red because of iron
oxide dust.

## Olympus Mons

Olympus Mons is the largest volcano in the solar system and the tallest
mountain on Mars. It is a shield volcano about 22 kilometres high.

## A day on Mars

A Martian day, called a sol, lasts about 24 hours and 37 minutes. A year on
Mars is roughly 687 Earth days.

## Dust storms

Mars is known for planet-wide dust storms that can hide the entire surface
for weeks at a time.
