# Brewing coffee

## Water temperature

The ideal water temperature for brewing coffee is between 92 and 96 degrees
Celsius. Water that is too hot scalds the grounds and tastes bitter.

## Grind size

Espresso needs a fine grind, almost like powder. A French press needs a coarse
grind. Pour-over sits between the two.

## Caffeine

Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors in the brain, which is why a cup of coffee
makes you feel more alert.
