# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to turn light into chemical energy.

## Where it happens

In plants, photosynthesis happens inside the chloroplasts of leaf cells. The
green pigment chlorophyll captures sunlight there.

## The gases

Plants absorb carbon dioxide from the air and release oxygen as a by-product.
This is why forests are often called the lungs of the planet.

## The light reaction

Light energy splits water molecules, producing oxygen and storing energy in
ATP and NADPH.
