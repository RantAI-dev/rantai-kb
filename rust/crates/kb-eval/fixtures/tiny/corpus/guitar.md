# Guitar chords

## Strings

A standard guitar has six strings. From the lowest to the highest they are E,
A, D, G, B and E.

## Barre chords

A barre chord is played by pressing one finger across several strings at the
same fret, so the finger acts like a movable nut. This lets one shape play any
chord in a key.

## Open chords

Open chords use unfretted strings and are the first chords most beginners
learn, such as C, G and D.
